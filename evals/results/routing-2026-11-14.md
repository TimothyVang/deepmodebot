## Method

Every call observed `openai-codex/gpt-6.1-sol` with `--max-cost 0.5`.

An earlier attempt from a non-TTY parent hung waiting on inherited stdin before `stdin=subprocess.DEVNULL` was added to `scripts/eval_routing.py`; its directory under `.scratch/routing-runs/` was retained rather than overwritten.

## Limits

- Tool-free `eval_routing.py` runs measure single-turn route and size selection from `SKILL.md` prose alone (`--no-tools`), not whether an agent invokes `tournament.py route` mid-turn or how 12 contender subagents perform downstream.
- All 24 cases in this batch used one model (`openai-codex/gpt-6.1-sol`) and a development-authored corpus; the prior 20-case baseline (`evals/results/routing-2026-11-12.json`) observed `anthropic/claude-opus-5-5`, so reported usage ($2.2895 vs $3.1019) is not a same-model cost comparison.
