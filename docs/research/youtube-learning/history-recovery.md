# Video-reference archive: coverage correction

Cataloged, captured, imported and reviewed are different states. Earlier collection work included substantial successful archives, but not every caption, image request or source import succeeded. The figures below preserve those distinctions; historical import counts are not a claim about current NotebookLM availability.

## What this recovery adds

Saved evidence now reconciles **58 source IDs: 49 with text artifacts and 9 unresolved**. This includes **29 fresh native-caption captures with 5,319 records**, plus **20 preserved historical texts**. Nonempty records are not synonymous with speech: 38 are bracket-only. Preserved texts were reused, not freshly captured or deeply reviewed.

The 42-source software-factory topic collection now accounts for its former 23 failure stubs through 22 fresh captures and one reused text. This is not a complete channel review or a guarantee of accurate endings. The new native captions for the Rasmic Svelte episode still end at a topic transition; its ending remains unresolved.

ZazenCodes now has a **376-item flat catalog: 131 regular videos and 245 Shorts**, with six existing transcript-text artifacts linked. Missing flat dates remain unknown; a saved extractor reports no streams tab. This does not mean all 376 transcripts were collected or read. Five selected source-board frames and a contact sheet supplement one source; overlays obscure parts, and the images are neither five distinct diagrams nor an exhaustive capture.
