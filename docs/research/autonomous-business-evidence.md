# How can an agent investigate a business-data opportunity without mistaking activity for proof?

Date: 2026-11-14. Feeds: Deepmode Bot's business-opportunity workflow.

## Answer

Test a narrow buyer workflow against source, rights, artifact, usefulness and economics evidence separately. Preserve unknowns and failed attempts; neither a dataset nor a polished report establishes demand or a sale. Evaluate the actual artifact and the validity of the steps, including data permissions, rather than accepting the agent's completion claim.

## Findings

- The [BizAgentBench preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20424v3) tests long-horizon business tasks against both goal attainment and execution validity; its simulated payments results show that goal completion can conceal invalid execution. This does not measure Deepmode Bot or establish business value.
- The [false-success study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09863) reports that agents can claim completion despite contradictory environment state and that its tested LLM judges struggled to detect this. Its benchmark results do not establish that a general detector will work for this workflow.
- In [Chen et al.'s report-revision study](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.609/), five research agents regressed on some previously covered content and citations during revision. The authors report that their tested prompt-engineering and dedicated-revision-agent approaches were insufficient; this does not prove that any proposed fix will work here.
- [Furnished Finder's Platform Terms of Use, sections 8 and 11](https://www.furnishedfinder.com/terms-of-use), last revised 2026-10-15, restrict platform use to personal, non-commercial purposes and expressly prohibit commercial use to obtain listing, traveler or contact information. Do not assume public visibility, a user account or a buyer's interest authorizes commercial collection or redistribution. Contract interpretation and any separately granted rights require the relevant agreement and, where needed, qualified advice.
- The [Census Bureau API terms](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/about/terms-of-service.html) permit services to retrieve and analyze its data subject to conditions, including restrictions on identifying respondents and an attribution notice. This is not permission to combine it with arbitrary restricted data or evidence that it answers a specific buyer's question.
- [CMS hospital data documentation](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/topics/hospitals/about-data/) describes Medicare-registered hospital characteristics and says U.S. government works are public domain; it asks for attribution and prohibits a false impression of government endorsement. Such a directory is not furnished-rental inventory or evidence of a buyer's demand.
- The [FTC's X-Mode/Outlogic action](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/01/ftc-order-prohibits-data-broker-x-mode-social-outlogic-selling-sensitive-location-data) illustrates risks from precise location data and inadequate downstream safeguards. It does not establish that a proposed housing-data workflow violates that order.

## Not settled

- The identity, authority, actual need, willingness to pay, and terms of the prospective buyer are user-reported or unknown; no buyer was contacted.
- No Furnished Finder listing data or customer data was collected. No commercial data rights, market valuation or product-market fit were established.
- A real authorized workflow, source coverage, cost and user outcome remain to be tested. Source hashes establish identity, not truth, legal permission or complete coverage.
