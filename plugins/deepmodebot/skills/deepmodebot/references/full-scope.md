# Full-scope completion

Applies to M/L and multi-item routes (Size **S** tracks its single item inline without a Scope Manifest or continuation loop). Slicing sequences verification; it never shrinks scope.

1. **Total Scope vs. Active Sprint.** Keep 100% of deliverables and inventory in ONE Scope Manifest (`docs/specs/<slug>-manifest.json` or `.scratch/goals/<slug>-manifest.json`, validated by `assets/loop/loop.py manifest`, mirrored in `todo`). Only the user removes scope; promises join it; an unqualified yes accepts offered options except hard stops. Rough ideas include essentials (persistence, validation, error/empty states, wired entrypoints). Complex/unclear domains clear **sme-review** (`sme_gate`) before Sprint 2+.
2. **Machine-checked inventory.** For "all/every/full set", classify every candidate in `"inventory"` as `included`, `duplicate` (`representative`), `non_target` (`reason`), or `blocked` (`evidence` + `unblock`). Any `pending` item fails `loop.py manifest`; batch without shrinking.
3. **Resolve reversible ambiguity.** Pick a scope-preserving structure from evidence, state assumptions, and isolate uncertain groups. Ask only when evidence cannot settle a material choice, an `sme_gate` is required, or a safety/permission boundary applies. Finish independent work first.
4. **Close against evidence.** Reconcile manifest, inventory, deliverables, and tasks before replying. Single-run builds drive `1/N` to `N/N`. Sprint mode (`active_sprint: 1` / `sme_gate: "required"`) lands Sprint 1 with proof and pauses ONLY at the sprint/SME checkpoint, reporting Sprint 2..N as remaining (`scope_complete: false`). Blockers name item, failure, attempts, and unblock path.
5. **Zero-loss resume.** Arm one continuation mechanism (`references/playbooks/run-a-goal.md`) and record its ID/state. On quota/cost cap, `checkpoint_every` / `sme_gate` (`loop.py run` exit `6`), or passing Sprint 1 with deferred slices (`exit 0`, `scope_complete: false`), manifest + `latest-summary.json` is the resume ledger. A pause is never completion.
